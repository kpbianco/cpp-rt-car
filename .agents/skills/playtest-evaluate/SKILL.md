---
name: playtest-evaluate
description: Plan and analyze game playtests using scenarios, telemetry, observation, and explicit human judgments without treating fun or balance as mechanically provable.
---

## Procedure
1. Define the player cohort, build, scenario, hypothesis, and instrumentation.
2. Verify deterministic gameplay invariants before the human session.
3. Capture completion, failure, pacing, economy, retention proxy, errors, exploits, and qualitative observations.
4. Separate measured behavior from player opinion and designer interpretation.
5. Recommend tuning experiments with expected signals and rollback.

## Exit criteria
Results identify what changed, what was observed, confidence, confounders, and the next experiment.
