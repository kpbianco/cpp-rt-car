---
name: product-intake
description: Normalize a business idea, product concept, or game concept into concrete product inputs and explicit assumptions.
---

## Inputs
- Business plan, concept note, or user description.
- Portfolio profile and known repository constraints.

## Procedure
1. Extract users, outcomes, value proposition, core workflow, constraints, risks, business model, and non-goals.
2. Separate facts, user decisions, assumptions, and open questions.
3. Identify validation that requires users, hardware, production data, or playtests.
4. Refuse to turn unresolved product choices into hidden implementation assumptions when they materially affect architecture.

## Outputs
- Updated business-plan.md.
- assumptions.md with confidence and validation method.
- Recommended delivery profile.

## Exit criteria
Inputs are specific enough to compile a product contract without inventing material product behavior.
