## Claim boundary

This batch establishes portable RT0 sampled-frame, deterministic policy,
acknowledged safe-output, and public software-loopback behavior over the
merged M21-03 coherent payload path. It does not establish physical DAC/DAQ/
CAN/IIO/XDMA/CUDA behavior, electrical scaling, physical trigger or sample
clock accuracy, HIL, controlled performance, RT1/RT2, Unreal, signing,
publication, or M21-05 replay/telemetry/overload closure. M21 and CAP-M21 remain
incomplete.

The end-to-end fixture registers three distinct rate domains, a two-reference
HAL-v2 device phase, one sampled actuator output and one sampled sensor input.
It proves exact startup-safe acknowledgement before running, CPU-produced
sequence 2/generation 1 materialization, loopback timestamp/calibration/
trigger rewrite, terminal-only input publication, inclusive maximum-age hold,
stale initial substitution, output-underrun safe substitution, and fixed read
metadata. A second device release proves the Runtime-owned destination
correlation template publishes device sequence 3/generation 2 instead of
retaining the differently rated CPU-output sequence. Duplicate publication
then proves deterministic overrun failure. Finally, one injected error during
checked stop leaves safety unknown and backend ownership retryable before the
repeated shutdown-safe request is acknowledged and the backend detaches.

During regression, an empty sampled plan initially allocated a direct map and
violated the exact logical extent ledger. The compiler now returns an exact
zero-storage plan when no sampled descriptors exist; both inherited M21-03
payload directions pass again. A second audit added every nonempty sampled
extent to the ledger, avoiding unaccounted runtime-owned storage.
