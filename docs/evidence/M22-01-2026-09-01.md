## Implemented boundary

- `LiveControlPolicy`, mailbox declarations, and producer declarations are
  copied only while configuring and frozen by successful finalization. Exact
  absolute mailbox, producer, record, payload-stride, payload-storage, and
  alignment ceilings are enforced with checked arithmetic. An all-zero policy
  disables the complete surface and preserves pre-M22 identity and accounting.
- Finalization allocates instance-local fixed record slots, Runtime-owned
  payload strides, producer sequences, counters, and an exact copy of legal
  compiled rate-release targets. Producer handles bind one non-reused Runtime
  identity, generation, mailbox, producer, and fixed producer index.
- `LiveControlUpdateRecord` is a fixed 128-byte schema-1 descriptor with closed
  target/update tables, canonical little-endian flag, exact payload extent and
  FNV-1a digest, and zero reserved fields. Host-frame and compiled-rate-release
  targets are structurally disjoint.
- Admission validates the complete descriptor before one atomic mailbox-claim
  attempt. It allocates, waits, spins, calls back, and performs I/O zero times.
  Contention returns `busy`; full capacity rejects new work without overwrite.
- An accepted update copies all payload bytes, zeroes the unused stride, writes
  the immutable record, and release-publishes the complete slot before
  occupancy, mailbox sequence, producer sequence, and accepted count advance.
- Read-only inspection exposes exact mailbox state, published records, and
  exact-size caller-owned payload copies without returning internal addresses
  or advancing execution.
- Stop closes admission first. If a producer already owns a bounded claim, the
  first checked stop returns `invalid_state`; retry after the claim quiesces
  completes stop. Published records remain inspectable until terminal Runtime
  destruction.
- Frozen schema/layout/legal tables, policy, capacity, admission/reset rules,
  and sorted mailbox/producer declarations enter graph, configuration, and
  replay compatibility identity. Staged bytes, arrival order, occupancy,
  counters, Runtime identity, and inspection state do not.
- Existing `MemoryPlan` runtime-control and planned-byte totals include copied
  declarations and every live-control allocation exactly once. No provider
  region, execution lane, consumer, checkpoint state, replay action, telemetry
  schema, or stable C ABI surface is added.

M22-01 never schedules, interprets, applies, checkpoints, replays, rolls back,
or emits telemetry for a staged update. Exact-boundary consumption and ordering
remain M22-02 work; rollback/replay/telemetry remain M22-03 work.
